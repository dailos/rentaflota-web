What people ask before signing

What does vehicle rental software cost?

Here, 2% of the volume you invoice, with tax outside the base. No setup fee, no monthly fee, no per-user licence and no charge per vehicle or location.

Is there a contract term or a monthly minimum?

No. The commission is calculated on what you invoice, so a slow month costs less and a month with nothing invoiced costs nothing.

Does it handle motorbikes and vans, or only cars?

All three, and commercial vehicles and motorhomes too. You define the categories, and they are what orders the public website.

Who owns the data?

You do. Your customers, your contracts and your history are yours, and you can take them with you, exported, if you ever stop working with us.

How long does it take to get running?

It depends on the size of the fleet and how many rates you have. The walkthrough is set up with your own vehicles and prices before you commit to anything.
