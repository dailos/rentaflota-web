Set the business up your way

No two rental firms are alike. The lists the whole system draws on are yours to define.

ExtrasChild seats, insurance, additional driver, whatever you sell. With its price, how it is charged and how many you have.
Vehicle featuresDoors, seats, gearbox, air conditioning, luggage. These are what show on the website and what customers choose by.
HolidaysThe days your office does not open, or opens differently. The website stops offering pick-ups when nobody is there.
PartnersWhoever sends you customers, with their commission and their code. Each one gets their own report by email, with nobody preparing it.
LocationsAs many as you like. Each with its opening hours, its address and its contribution measured separately.
UsersFour roles with different permissions, so the front desk cannot reach what belongs to the owner.
Cost categoriesYours, so that fleet costs add up under headings that mean something to you.
