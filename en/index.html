What it covers

This is not a booking module with extras bolted on. It is the whole business, including the website that sells.

The website that sells

Your public site, search box first, in four languages, taking payment in two screens. Not a separate website you then have to wire up.

The front desk

The day's planning, plate assignment, the contract, customer check-in and the till closing.

The fleet

Plate by plate: what state each vehicle is in, what it has earned, what it has cost and when it is due for service.

Compliance

The police reporting Spanish law requires of every rental goes out on its own, with nobody having to remember.

Accounting

Eleven reports with Excel exports, a daily till closing, and invoices flowing into your accounting software by themselves.

Getting found

Landing pages, a blog in every language, vouchers and automatic replies to reviews.

And artificial intelligence across all of it

It helps staff with the contract paperwork, answers your Google reviews and looks after customers on the website all the way to a finished booking.

Two screens that stand in for the rest

The day, as a grid

One model per row, one day per column. At a glance you can see what is free, what goes out today and what has to come back.

The fleet, vehicle by vehicle

Every plate with its state, its mileage, when it was bought and where it is parked. Finding one particular vehicle means typing its plate.

The contract, everything in front of you

Customer, vehicle, dates, amount and the state of the rental across the header. Below it, on the same screen, the payments, the documents, the invoice and the police report.
