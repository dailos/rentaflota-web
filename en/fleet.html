Advanced fleet management

You do not manage models, you manage plates. Every vehicle has its own state, its own costs, its own reminders and its own bottom line.

The state of every vehicle

Four states, and the whole system behaves accordingly: availability, the calendar, the reports and the public website.

Active

Rentable. Counts towards availability and shows on the website.

Blocked

In the workshop or held back for something. Cannot be rented on those dates, but it is still yours.

Sold

Out of the fleet from the date of sale. Stops counting forward and keeps counting in the past.

Given out

Handed over as a courtesy vehicle. Out of the fleet, exactly like a sold one.

And how many of each

The fleet filters by state, with the count beside it. Knowing how many vehicles are sitting in the workshop is reading a number, not counting by hand.

What each vehicle has actually earned

A profit and loss account for one plate, not for the model: what it brought in, what it cost, the balance, the days it spent rented, what it returns per hundred kilometres, and how long until it pays for itself.

The plate's own page

Everything that has happened to that specific vehicle, starting with what it is: model, state, mileage, purchase date, chassis number and where it is.

  • History of assigned bookings, with what each one earned
  • Costs, sorted into your own categories
  • The vehicle's files, from the purchase to the last repair

The vehicle keeps the reminders, not somebody's memory

An alert fires at a given mileage or on a given date, says what it is about, and arms itself again every so many kilometres or every so many months.

The rest of the fleet screen

Advanced searchBy plate, model, category, state, location or mileage.
Service alertsYou get warned at a given mileage or on a given date, and the warning repeats every so many kilometres or every so many months.
CostsEvery cost belongs to a plate and to a category, and lowers that vehicle's balance.
Models and categoriesModels are grouped into whatever categories your business uses, and that grouping is what orders the public website.
